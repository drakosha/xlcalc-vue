# PivotTables

A workbook's PivotTables show on the sheet as their cells, as Excel last laid them out. The sheet knows where each one lies, refreshes the one under the active cell, shows Excel's alert for what a PivotTable's cells do not take, creates a PivotTable with Excel's Insert PivotTable ([Insert PivotTable](#insert-pivottable)) and changes its fields in Excel's PivotTable Fields pane ([The PivotTable Fields pane](#the-pivottable-fields-pane)). Excel's dialogs and menus over a PivotTable are the sheet's: its cell menu, the filter buttons on its headers, Value Field Settings, Field Settings, PivotTable Options, Grouping, Insert Calculated Field and Item, and the PivotTable Analyze and Design commands ([Dialogs over a PivotTable](#dialogs-over-a-pivottable)). The source's `structure` takes all of xlcalc's operations on PivotTables ([Operations](#operations)).

## Where the PivotTables are

A source lists each sheet's PivotTables in `SheetInfo.pivots`, read with the sheets and again on `reload` or `sheets`. Each is a `SheetPivot`, its name and its areas as ranges:

| Field | Area |
| --- | --- |
| `name` | The PivotTable's name, unique among the sheet's (`PivotTable1`): an operation names the PivotTable by its sheet and this name. |
| `range` | The table without its Filters area (Excel's `TableRange1`). |
| `filters` | The Filters area above the table, as wide as its filters, without the blank row between (Excel's `PageRange`). |
| `headers` | The table's rows above its values: the value fields' and column fields' headers and the column labels. |
| `rowLabels` | The row labels left of the values, down to the grand total. |
| `values` | The values with their totals (Excel's `DataBodyRange`). A PivotTable without value fields has it too, as Excel gives it: the cells under the header rows, which are the row labels themselves with no field on columns, the column labels with no field on rows, the empty area of a PivotTable with no field. |

`@xlcalc/vue/local` takes them from xlcalc's `PivotLocation.areas` (`table`, `page`, `columnHeaders`, `rowLabels`, `data`), which xlcalc works out on every PivotTable it gives. A PivotTable not laid out (added without the engine: Excel's empty PivotTable of three columns and 18 rows) or shrunk to its top-left cell, which shows `#SPILL!`, has no `headers` or `rowLabels`. A PivotTable laid out again (Refresh, a label renamed, an undo) is listed anew as the sheet reads the sheets again.

The sheet's `state` event names the PivotTable under the active cell, its Filters area included, as `pivot`, so that a ribbon can show Excel's PivotTable Analyze and Design tabs while the active cell is in one; `canRefreshPivot` says that Refresh is available there, which it is not on a protected sheet ([Protection](#protection)).

## Refresh and Refresh All

Excel's PivotTable Analyze > Refresh (Alt+F5) reads the PivotTable's source again and lays out every PivotTable over the same pivot cache. The sheet sends it to the source's `structure` as xlcalc's `refreshPivots` naming the PivotTable under the active cell: by Alt+F5 on the grid, by Refresh in the cell menu of a PivotTable's cell, by `SheetViewApi.refreshPivot()` for a ribbon, and by Refresh in the toolbar's Refresh All list; on a protected sheet none of them refreshes ([Protection](#protection)). It is one step of undo, as xlcalc makes it; the sheet reads its windows again afterwards. A PivotTable keeps its values while its source changes, until a refresh, as in Excel.

Excel's Data > Refresh All (Ctrl+Alt+F5) refreshes the PivotTables too. It is the source's `refreshConnections` without `names`, so the sheet, `SheetViewApi.refreshAll()` and the toolbar send it as before ([calculation.md](calculation.md#refresh-all)). In `@xlcalc/vue/local` it asks the calls outside the workbook again first, when the host answers them (`externals.resolve`), then refreshes every pivot cache the workbook has then (xlcalc's `refreshPivots` without `pivots`, which passes over a cache over another workbook, a connection or the data model). The PivotTables are refreshed as soon as the calls return: an answer that arrives later (a cell that showed `#BUSY!` or `#GETTING_DATA`) reaches its cell, not the PivotTables over it, until the next refresh. Every `./local` source has Refresh All, and the workbook's layout at the call tells whether there are PivotTables to refresh; without them and without a resolver it changes nothing. Asking the calls again is not a step of undo, as in Excel; the PivotTables' refresh is one step, as xlcalc's. `refreshConnections` with `names` asks those functions' calls and leaves the PivotTables.

The toolbar's `refreshAll` group is a split button, as Excel's Refresh All on the Data tab: the button refreshes all, and its list holds Refresh All and Refresh, the latter enabled while Refresh is available (`state.canRefreshPivot`). Its texts are `refreshAll`, `refreshAllTip`, `refreshAllMenu`, `refreshAllItem`, `menuRefresh` (the list's and the cell menu's Refresh) and `refreshPivot` (Refresh's tooltip with its key) ([toolbar.md](toolbar.md)).

## Insert PivotTable

Excel's Insert > PivotTable opens its dialog, PivotTable from table or range: `SheetViewApi.insertPivotTable()` for a ribbon, and the toolbar's `pivotTable` group. The Table/Range box starts with what Excel puts there: the name of the table at the active cell, otherwise the selection when it is more than one cell, otherwise the active cell's current region as Excel finds it for Create Table, written with its sheet (`Data!$A$1:$E$40`); empty for an empty cell alone. The box takes a range with its sheet or without (the active sheet's), a table's name or a defined name. Below it New Worksheet (chosen) and Existing Worksheet with its Location box, a cell or a range whose top-left cell the PivotTable starts at.

OK sends xlcalc's `addPivotTable` through the source's `structure`, one step of undo: on a new sheet named as the sheet names a new one (`Sheet4`), inserted before the active sheet, at A3 as Excel puts it; or at the location. The sheet then shows the PivotTable's sheet with its top-left cell selected, where the PivotTable Fields pane shows. A source the box does not take keeps the dialog open with Excel's alert ("Data source reference is not valid."), and so does a location that is no range of the workbook's sheets ("Destination reference is not valid."); the source's refusal (a place over another PivotTable, a source with no row under its headers) is shown as the sheet shows any refusal, with xlcalc's reason. Over cells that hold something or a table the PivotTable is made all the same and shows `#SPILL!` in its top-left cell until a refresh finds its place free, as Excel 365 makes it without asking (xlcalc 0.72). The dialog has no external data source and no Data Model box, which xlcalc does not take. Its texts are `pivotCreateTitle`, `pivotCreateSource`, `pivotCreateRange`, `pivotCreatePlace`, `pivotNewSheet`, `pivotExistingSheet`, `pivotLocation`, `pivotSourceBad` and `pivotLocationBad`; the toolbar's button is `pivotInsert`. They are Excel 16.0 for Windows' words, measured in English and in Russian (`Сводная таблица из таблицы или диапазона`, `Новый лист`), except the two alerts, which were not measured.

## The PivotTable Fields pane

Excel shows its PivotTable Fields pane while the active cell is in a PivotTable and hides it outside one; so does the sheet, to the right of the grid (below it on a phone), while the source has `pivotTable` and the Field List is on: `state.fieldList`. Excel's PivotTable Analyze > Field List is `SheetViewApi.fieldList(show?)`, on by default; the pane's close button turns it off, and the cell menu of a PivotTable's cell has Show Field List or Hide Field List, last, as Excel's. A host that places the pane itself sets `SheetView`'s `pivotFields` to false and draws `PivotFieldsPane` with the sheet's `source`, its template ref as `sheet` and its `state`, while `state.fieldList` is on. `--xs-pf-w` and `--xs-pf-h` size the sheet's own.

The pane reads the PivotTable through the source's `pivotTable(sheet, name)`, the definition as xlcalc holds it with its pivot cache's fields (`SheetPivotTable`), and reads it again on the source's `reload` and `sheets` events, so an undo or a Refresh shows in it. Each reading carries the whole definition with every cache field's items: for a source over a network (`./remote`) that is the traffic of the pane on each such event while it is shown. From the top, in Excel's default layout (Fields Section and Areas Section Stacked):

- Choose fields to add to report: each field of the cache with a box, checked while the field is in an area, its name in bold; Search filters the list by the text it contains. A field filtered on the PivotTable (an item hidden, a filter by label, value or date) has Excel's funnel. Checking a field puts it where Excel for Windows' field list puts it, as xlcalc's `suggestPivotField` says (measured in Excel's field list): a field of numbers alone, blanks allowed, onto Values, summarized by Sum; any other onto Rows, numbers mixed with text, logicals, errors or dates, numbers stored as text and blanks alone included. A field dragged onto Values is summarized by the function `suggestPivotField` names, Sum for a field of numbers, Count otherwise. A field of dates checked, or put on Rows or Columns, is grouped as Excel 365's field list groups it (xlcalc's `groupDates`, in the same step of undo): dates over several years by Years, Quarters and Months with the dates, collapsed to the years; within one year by Months and Days; within one month not at all. Unchecking it removes it from every area. A field's arrow, shown on hover, asks for its sort and filter menu ([Dialogs the pane asks for](#dialogs-the-pane-asks-for)); a right click on a field, or Shift+F10 or the menu key on its box, opens Excel's Add to Report Filter, Add to Row Labels, Add to Column Labels and Add to Values.
- Drag fields between areas below: Filters, Columns, Rows and Values, each listing its fields in order. A field is dragged from the list onto an area, or from an area onto another or to another place in its own; a line shows where it lands. A field lies on one axis, so dragged onto Filters, Columns or Rows it leaves the axis it was on; a value field dragged there leaves Values. A field dragged onto Values becomes a value field, Sum for numbers alone and Count for anything else (blanks too), as Excel summarizes a new one, and xlcalc names it as Excel does (`Sum of Amount`, `Count of Region`, `Sum of Amount2` for a second); dragged there from an axis it leaves the axis, from the list it stays where it is. A field dragged out of the areas is removed, as in Excel; Esc ends a drag without a change.
- Σ Values stands for the value fields on Columns (or on Rows) once there are two of them, as in Excel: xlcalc's `editPivotTable` puts it on Columns at the end with the second value field, takes it away with the second to last and brings it back where it was, so the pane shows it as the source answers (with Defer Layout Update on, after Update). It is dragged between Columns and Rows only, which lays the value fields across the columns or down the rows (xlcalc's `values` among the axis's fields).
- A click on a field in an area, or Enter or Space on it, opens Excel's menu of the field: Move Up, Move Down, Move to Beginning, Move to End; Move to Report Filter, Move to Row Labels, Move to Column Labels, Move to Values; Remove Field; and Field Settings… (Value Field Settings… on Values), which the pane asks the sheet for. The arrows, Home and End move in the menu, Esc closes it. Σ Values has no Field Settings and goes neither to Filters nor to Values.
- Defer Layout Update keeps the changes in the pane, without laying the PivotTable out, until Update; unchecking it lays them out too, as Excel does. A change made while Update is on its way waits for the next Update; a change of the PivotTable from elsewhere (an undo, a Refresh) drops what waited, which was built on the old layout. Another PivotTable starts with it unchecked. While changes wait, Value Field Settings… is offered only for a value field the PivotTable already has, since the host's dialog reads the PivotTable as the source holds it.

Each change is one `editPivotTable` through the sheet's `structure` with `groupDates`, one step of undo: the areas it changes set whole (`rows`, `columns`, `pages`, `values`), and an Update after Defer Layout Update sends every change since in one. The pane shows the change at once, and a change made before the source answers builds on it, each sent after the one before; once the last is answered the pane shows the PivotTable as the source laid it out, and after a refusal as it stayed. A menu's item acts on its field where it is when chosen. The pane only shows the PivotTable, its boxes and menus disabled, where the sheet takes no change of it: without `sheet`, without the source's `structure`, on a read-only sheet (where `state.canInsertPivot` is absent), and on a protected sheet without Use PivotTable and PivotChart. Its texts are `fieldListTitle`, `fieldListChoose`, `fieldListSearch`, `fieldListDrag`, `areaFilters`, `areaColumns`, `areaRows`, `areaValues`, `fieldListValues`, `fieldListFilter` (the arrow's accessible name), `fieldListDefer`, `fieldListUpdate`, the menu's `fieldMoveUp`, `fieldMoveDown`, `fieldMoveFirst`, `fieldMoveLast`, `fieldToFilters`, `fieldToRows`, `fieldToColumns`, `fieldToValues`, `fieldRemove`, `fieldSettings`, `valueFieldSettings`, the list's `fieldAddFilters`, `fieldAddRows`, `fieldAddColumns`, `fieldAddValues`, and the cell menu's `menuShowFieldList` and `menuHideFieldList`. They are Excel 16.0 for Windows' words, measured in English and in Russian (`Поля сводной таблицы`, `Отложить обновление макета`, `Параметры полей значений...`), except `fieldListValues`, `fieldListFilter`, the list's menu and `menuShowFieldList`, which are Microsoft's documentation's or Excel 365 for Windows'.

The pane does not have Excel's Tools menu (the other layouts of the sections, sorting the list A to Z) or the Values area's field filter buttons on the sheet's headers.

### Dialogs the pane asks for

Field Settings…, Value Field Settings… and a field's sort and filter menu are asked for through `SheetViewApi.pivotDialog(request)`: `PivotDialogRequest`, the dialog (`fieldSettings`, `valueFieldSettings`, `fieldFilter`), the PivotTable's sheet and name, the cache field's index (`field`), for a value field its index in the PivotTable's `values` as the source holds it (`value`), which a change waiting for Update does not shift, and where the pane's control is (`at`), where the filter menu opens. The sheet opens its own dialog for each ([Dialogs over a PivotTable](#dialogs-over-a-pivottable)); a request of a kind it does not know goes on to the host as the `pivotDialog` event.

## Dialogs over a PivotTable

Excel's commands over a PivotTable are the sheet's while the source has `structure`, `pivotTable` and `pivotCells` and the sheet is editable, with Use PivotTable and PivotChart on a protected sheet: `state.canEditPivot`. Each OK is one xlcalc operation through `structure`, one step of undo, after which the sheet and the pane read the PivotTable again: `editPivotTable` for what the PivotTable itself holds, `groupPivotField` and `ungroupPivotField`, `setPivotCalculatedField` and `setPivotCalculatedItem`, `renamePivotTable`, `deletePivotTable`. The labels are Excel 16.0 for Windows', measured in its en-US and ru-RU interfaces, but for the filters' dialogs, the Sort dialog, the months and quarters of All Dates in the Period and the Design tab's lists, which follow Microsoft's documentation.

### What a cell is

The source's `pivotCells(sheet, name, cells?)` tells what a PivotTable's cells show as it was last laid out: its fields' items as labels (a caption, or the value in the field's number format, a date as the short date), the cells with Excel's filter buttons, and what each cell asked for is, xlcalc's `PivotCell` as Excel's `Range.PivotCell` tells it: a value (`value`, with the items of its row and column), an item's label, a subtotal's or a grand total's (`item`, `subtotal`, `grand`), a value field's name (`valueName`), a field's name over its labels or in the Filters area (`field`), Σ Values (`valuesField`), a Filters area's item (`pageItem`), or none of them (`blank`, Row Labels and Column Labels too, which name no field). Every cell of a line's labels is its item's, every cell of a total's line the total's; the cells between the filters and the blank row under them are of no PivotTable. `@xlcalc/vue/local` takes the cells and the buttons from xlcalc's `pivotCells` (0.73, measured on Excel's `Range.PivotCell`), the labels, the value fields' formats and the calculated fields and items from the PivotTable and its cache; the sheet reads them for the displayed sheet's PivotTables as it reads the sheets, and for the active cell as it moves.

Removing a value field and adding a calculated field leave Σ Values on an axis as Excel leaves it, which xlcalc's operations keep. Where the source has `pivotTable` but not `pivotCells` or `structure`, the pane's requests go on to the host as the `pivotDialog` event. The filter buttons, Alt+Down, Group and Ungroup follow `state.canEditPivot`, as the dialogs do.

### The cell menu

The cell menu of a PivotTable's cell lists Excel's commands after Paste, as Excel for Windows does:

- on a value or a value field's name: Number Format… (Format Cells with the Number tab alone, the value field's format), Refresh, Delete PivotTable, Remove "Sum of Amount", Summarize Values By (Sum, Count, Average, Max, Min, Product, More Options…), Show Values As (the 15 calculations, those that take a base field opening Value Field Settings, More Options…), Value Field Settings… and PivotTable Options…; the item in force is checked;
- on a row or column field's label or header: Refresh, Delete PivotTable, Sort (Sort A to Z, Sort Z to A, for dates Sort Oldest to Newest and Newest to Oldest, More Sort Options…), Filter (Clear Filter From "Region", Keep Only Selected Items, Hide Selected Items, Top 10…, Label Filters…, Value Filters…), Subtotal "Region" checked while the field has subtotals, Group…, Ungroup…, Remove "Region", Field Settings… and PivotTable Options…;
- on a field of the Filters area: Refresh, Delete PivotTable, Remove, Field Settings… and PivotTable Options….

A submenu opens beside its item on a pointer over it, a click, Right arrow or Enter; Left arrow closes it.

### The filter menu

Excel's filter button stands at the bottom right of the cells where xlcalc's `pivotCells` says Excel draws it (0.74, measured on Excel for Windows' screen): Row Labels and Column Labels, each field's name over its labels and a Filters area field's item, none with Display field captions and filter drop downs off. A button on a field's name or a Filters area's item filters that field; Row Labels and Column Labels, which name no field, filter their axis (xlcalc's `axis`), the menu starting at the axis's first field; a button on Σ Values' name or over an axis that holds Σ Values alone opens nothing, and only a button with a field shows the funnel. Their cell menus are those of the axis's first field, as the sheet had them before xlcalc named these cells `blank` (Excel's menu there was not measured); a field filtered shows a funnel. A click, Alt+Down on the cell, or the arrow beside a field in the PivotTable Fields pane opens the field's menu:

- Select field (in compact form with several fields on the axis), Sort A to Z, Sort Z to A (Oldest to Newest and Newest to Oldest for dates), More Sort Options… (the Sort dialog: Manual, Ascending or Descending by the field's labels or a value field's values, its Summary), Clear Filter From;
- Label Filters (Date Filters for a field of dates) and Value Filters, each with Clear Filter and its comparisons, Value Filters with Top 10…, Date Filters with the periods from Tomorrow to Year to Date, All Dates in the Period (Quarter 1 to 4, January to December) and Custom Filter…; a comparison opens its dialog (Label Filter, Value Filter with the value field it tests, Date Filter with Whole Days), filled from the field's filter of that kind;
- Search and the items' list with (Select All); OK hides the items unchecked, and with a search every item it leaves out too, one item staying shown at least. A Filters area's field lists (All) and its items, or with Select Multiple Items boxes to check.

A label or date filter takes the place of the field's items unchecked and of its other filters; a value filter or Top 10 that of its other value filter; with Allow multiple filters per field (PivotTable Options) the label or date filter and the value filter stay together, as xlcalc applies them. Typed numbers and dates are read in the workbook's locale.

### Value Field Settings and Field Settings

Value Field Settings shows the source field's name, Custom Name, the tab Summarize Values By (Excel's 11 functions) and Show Values As (its 15 calculations, Base field among the source's fields and Base item with (previous) and (next), enabled for the calculations that take them) and Number Format. Picking another function changes Custom Name at once, as Excel 16.0 for Windows does (measured in en-US and ru-RU): a name that begins with the old function's prefix (`Sum of `, `Сумма по полю `, in any case) gets the new one (`Count of `) and keeps the rest as typed; any other name becomes the new function's name of the source field (`Count of Amount`). The rule is xlcalc's `renamedValueField` (0.73). A name typed before the pick is overwritten, one typed after it is kept, and OK saves what the box holds. Summarize Values By in the cell menu sends the new function without a name, and xlcalc's `editPivotTable` names the value field by the same rule. Picking another base field starts its base item again at (previous), the list's first entry (not measured in Excel). Number Format keeps a built-in format's id when OK leaves the code as it was.

Field Settings of a row, column or Filters area field shows its Custom Name, Subtotals & Filters (Automatic, None, Custom with its functions; Include new items in manual filter) and Layout & Print (Show item labels in outline form with compact form and subtotals at the top, or in tabular form; Repeat item labels; Insert blank line after each item label; Show items with no data; Insert page break after each item), set on the field (`PivotField`).

### PivotTable Options

PivotTable Options shows the tabs of Excel's dialog the workbook models: the name; Layout & Format (Merge and center cells with labels, the compact form's indent, the Filters area's order and fields per column or row, For error values show, For empty cells show, Autofit column widths on update, Preserve cell formatting on update); Totals & Filters (the grand totals for rows and columns, Subtotal filtered page items, Allow multiple filters per field, Use Custom Lists when sorting); Display (Show expand/collapse buttons, Display field captions and filter drop downs, Classic PivotTable layout, Show the Values row); Data (Enable show details); Alt Text. OK sends what changed in one `editPivotTable`, and a new name as `renamePivotTable`, a step of its own.

### Grouping

Group… on an item of a field of dates or numbers, and PivotTable Analyze's Group Field, open Excel's Grouping dialog: Auto with Starting at and Ending at (the least and greatest value, a field of whole days ending a day after its last date, as Excel proposes them; typing in a box clears its Auto), and By: the periods from Seconds to Years (Months by default) with Number of days for Days alone, or the numbers' interval (10 by default). Group… over several items of one field, as Group Selection, gathers them into a group by hand (`Group1`). Ungroup… takes away the groups selected of a field grouped by hand, or the grouping of a field of dates or numbers; it is offered only on such a field, as xlcalc ungroups, not on the field a group by hand was made of. Number of days that is not a whole number from 1 on keeps the dialog open with an alert, whose words were not measured. A group is renamed by typing over its label, as xlcalc takes it. Excel's refusals ("Cannot group that selection") come back with xlcalc's words.

### Calculated fields and items

PivotTable Analyze > Fields, Items, & Sets has Calculated Field… and Calculated Item…: Excel's Insert Calculated Field and Insert Calculated Item in "Region" (the active cell's field), with Name (Field1, Formula1 at first, the existing ones listed), Formula (`= 0`), the fields and the field's items with Insert Field and Insert Item, Add (Modify for an existing name), Delete, OK and Close. Formulas are typed and shown in the workbook's locale (`=Amount*0,5`, `=ЕСЛИ(…;…;…)` in ru-RU): the source's `pivotCells` gives the existing ones so, and the sheet reads a typed one with xlcalc's entry parsing into the form its operations take; a formula the parsing does not take (a field's name in quotes) goes as typed. OK adds or changes the calculated field or item; a new calculated field is put on Values after the others with Sum (`Sum of Double`), Σ Values on Columns from the second value field, as Excel for Windows' OK puts it, in the same step of undo: xlcalc's `setPivotCalculatedField` with `values`. Modify leaves the value fields as they are.

### PivotTable Analyze and Design

`SheetViewApi.pivotCommand(command)` opens PivotTable Options, Insert Calculated Field, Insert Calculated Item or Grouping (Group Field); `groupPivot()` is Group Selection and `ungroupPivot()` Ungroup; `pivotDesign(change)` is a command of the Design tab: Subtotals (Do Not Show Subtotals, Show all Subtotals at Bottom or at Top of Group, set on every field of the rows and columns), Grand Totals (off, on, on for rows only or for columns only: Excel's Show grand totals for rows and for columns, RowGrand and ColumnGrand; RowGrand is the grand total column at the right, xlsx `colGrandTotals`, xlcalc's `grandTotals.columns`, and ColumnGrand the row at the bottom, `grandTotals.rows`, as xlcalc's docs/development.md records Excel for Windows writing them, the case `no-row-grand`), Report Layout (a form for the PivotTable and every field, Repeat All Item Labels, Do Not Repeat Item Labels) and Blank Rows (on every row field). The toolbar's `pivotAnalyze` and `pivotDesign` groups draw them while the active cell is in a PivotTable, the Design lists checking the item in force.

## What its cells take

xlcalc does not let an entry change a PivotTable's values, as Excel does not. Excel 16.0 for Windows refuses at the first key typed on a value cell, before Enter, so the sheet does too: a key typed, or F2, on a cell of a PivotTable's `values` area (`SheetInfo.pivots`) that is no label (in neither `rowLabels` nor `headers`: a value, or an empty cell of a PivotTable without value fields) shows Excel's alert, "We can't change this part of the PivotTable." (`pivotPartAlert`), and opens no editor; a label takes typing, which renames it, and so does the one cell of a PivotTable in `#SPILL!`, as xlcalc takes it. The sheet shows the source's refusal (`EditOutcome` code `pivot`) as an alert, for what the sheet sends otherwise and for a host that sends an edit itself. `@xlcalc/vue/local` takes the kind of Excel's alert from the `reason` of xlcalc's refusal, or of the `PivotInputError` xlcalc throws for a cleared cell, and words it as Excel for Windows does, in the workbook's language:

- a value typed over a value or an empty cell of it, or entered with Ctrl+Enter with the active cell there: "We can't change this part of the PivotTable." (`part`);
- a formula over its cells, a label or a value: "Cannot enter a formula for an item or field name in a PivotTable report." (`formula`). The sheet never sends a formula over a value, refused at the first key; a host that sends one with `edit` gets this alert, as Excel's object model answered it (xlcalc's set `alerts-held`);
- Delete, Clear All over part of it, texts entered into several of its cells at once, a paste, and inserting or deleting rows, columns or cells through it, moving part of it, a range copied or moved onto it, filling or merging over it (`affect`): "We can't make this change for the selected cells because it will affect a PivotTable. Use the field list to change the report. If you are trying to insert or delete cells, move the PivotTable and try again."

The alerts are Excel 16.0 for Windows' words, measured in its interface in English and in Russian (where `переместить сводную таблицу` is Excel's own), and the English ones are also those Excel answered when xlcalc measured these entries through its object model (its set `edit`). A range copied or moved onto a PivotTable takes the alert Excel's object model gives it, as xlcalc measured. Every other refusal, which xlcalc gives without a `reason`, keeps xlcalc's words: a value field named as a field of the source ("PivotTable field name already exists"), a Filters area's item it does not have ("is not an item of this field"), Refresh of a source that is gone. Delete or Clear over the whole PivotTable (its table and its Filters area) deletes it, as Excel does, in one step of undo, and the sheet reads the sheets again. Text typed over a label renames it, as in Excel and as xlcalc does: an item's label gives the item a caption, a field's name, a value field's name, `Grand Total` or `Row Labels` their names, a Filters area's item shows that item.

## Protection

Excel 16.0 for Windows refuses to refresh a PivotTable on a protected sheet whatever the sheet allows, as measured in its interface in English and in Russian. Without Use PivotTable and PivotChart (`SheetProtection.pivotTables`) its PivotTable Analyze and Design tabs are gone; with it Refresh on PivotTable Analyze is disabled. Either way Alt+F5 does nothing, without an alert, the cell menu's Refresh is disabled, and Refresh All (Ctrl+Alt+F5, Data > Refresh All) shows an alert and leaves the PivotTable as it was. The sheet does the same: `state.canRefreshPivot` is absent, Alt+F5 does nothing, the cell menu's and the toolbar list's Refresh are disabled, and `refreshPivot()` returns null. `@xlcalc/vue/local` refuses a refresh (`refreshPivots`, and Refresh All's refresh of the PivotTables after it asked the calls outside the workbook) when a sheet with a PivotTable over the cache, or any sheet with one for Refresh All, is protected, with Excel's alert (`EditOutcome` code `protected`): without Use PivotTable and PivotChart "Cannot edit PivotTable on protected sheet." (`Нельзя изменять "сводной таблицы" на защищенном листе.`), with it "That command cannot be performed while a protected sheet contains another PivotTable report based on the same source data." and its second paragraph, "To remove protection from the sheet that has the other report, click the sheet tab, and then click Unprotect Sheet (Review tab, Protect group). Then try the command again.", which Excel shows though the sheet holds that one PivotTable alone.

For the rest, Use PivotTable and PivotChart ("Use PivotTable reports" in Microsoft's Protect a worksheet) lets users "Format, change the layout, refresh, or otherwise modify PivotTable reports, or create new reports", and Edit objects (`objects`) lets them change objects not unlocked before the sheet was protected; slicers and timelines, the article says, "are already locked when you add them". `@xlcalc/vue/local` follows these words, not measured in Excel:

- Every operation on a PivotTable needs Use PivotTable and PivotChart on each sheet whose PivotTables it lays out again: a change of the cache (grouping, calculated fields and items) every PivotTable over the cache, a move both sheets.
- A slicer's or a timeline's choice and its Report Connections refilter PivotTables: the box on the sheets of the PivotTables it filters, and of those it connects.
- Adding a slicer or a timeline, and moving, resizing or deleting a locked one, needs Edit objects on its sheet too ([slicers.md](slicers.md#protection)).
- Text typed over a label renames it, a change of the PivotTable: besides an unlocked cell, it needs the box.

Without them the source refuses with Excel's protected sheet alert (`EditOutcome` code `protected`). The alert Excel shows for these was not measured, and Excel's own refusal may differ from Microsoft's description.

## Operations

The source's `structure` takes every operation of xlcalc on PivotTables, slicers and timelines (`addPivotTable`, `editPivotTable`, `renamePivotTable`, `movePivotTable`, `deletePivotTable`, `refreshPivots`, `groupPivotField`, `ungroupPivotField`, `renamePivotGroup`, `setPivotCalculatedField`, `setPivotCalculatedItem`, `addSlicer`, `editSlicer`, `deleteSlicer`, `addTimeline`, `editTimeline`, `deleteTimeline`), each one step of undo, so the sheet's Insert PivotTable and PivotTable Fields pane, and a host's own dialogs, send them through `SheetViewApi.structure` and the sheet reads the sheets again afterwards. xlcalc's guide to PivotTables (its `docs/pivot-tables.md`) describes them. In `@xlcalc/vue/local` the engine is loaded when the workbook opens with PivotTables, otherwise before the first operation on PivotTables ([local.md](local.md#pivottables)), so a PivotTable added to a workbook without them is laid out at once. Where the engine cannot be loaded (its chunk fails to download), these operations, Refresh and the PivotTables' part of Refresh All refuse with the source's own message (`pivot`), "PivotTables can't be refreshed or changed now: this page could not load what they need. Reload the page and try again.", and the workbook stays as it is.

## Files

`openXlsx` reads a file's PivotTables, caches, slicers and timelines, and `saveXlsx` writes them; what the workbook does not hold of them (a part of a cache or a PivotTable, PivotTables the model cannot take, which xlcalc words as `pivotUnread`, and a cache's source on a deleted sheet, written as Excel writes it, `pivotSourceLinked`) is reported under the issue code `pivotTable`, which `groupIssues` groups as the others: one group, with the first issue's message. Writing over the original file (`over`) rejects slicers and timelines added or deleted with xlcalc's `KeptRefusal` `code: 'slicers'` ([local.md](local.md#files)).

## Not yet

The PivotTable's style is kept for Excel and not drawn: its cells show their own formats only. Slicers and timelines are drawn and used on the sheet, with Insert Slicer and Insert Timeline ([slicers.md](slicers.md)).

Of Excel's commands over a PivotTable the sheet does not have: Expand/Collapse and Show Details; Move; Sort on a value (Sort Smallest to Largest by the column clicked); Summarize Values By's Distinct Count, which needs the data model; PivotTable Options' Printing tab, Show contextual tooltips, Show properties in tooltips, Show items with no data on rows and columns, Display item labels when no fields are in the values area, the Field List's order, and its Data tab's Save source data with file, Refresh data when opening the file and Number of items to retain per field, which belong to the pivot cache and no operation of xlcalc changes; Solve Order and List Formulas; the Design tab's styles and their options.
