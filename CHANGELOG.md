# Changelog

## Unreleased

Built over xlcalc 0.60.0. Nothing breaks for a host; Paste Special and Format Painter in `./local` need xlcalc 0.60.0.

- Paste Special: the cell menu's Paste Special…, Ctrl+Alt+V (Ctrl+Cmd+V on a Mac) and `SheetViewApi.pasteSpecial()` open Excel's dialog for a copy: All, Formulas, Values, Formats or Validation, and Transpose, through `structure`'s `copyRange` with `paste` and `transpose` (xlcalc 0.60). The cell menu has the new item after Paste.
- Format Painter: the toolbar's `formatPainter` group (with `sheet`; in `TOOLBAR_GROUPS` after `undo`, so a host with the default items gets the button) and `SheetViewApi.formatPainter(mode)`: a click paints the selection's formats onto the next range once, a double click until Esc, through `copyRange` with `paste: 'formats'`. `SheetViewState` has `canFormatPainter` and `painting`; `FormatPainterMode` is new.
- The display filter for a ribbon: `SheetViewApi.filterColumn()`, `clearFilter()` and `sortColumn('asc' | 'desc')`, and `SheetViewState.filtered`.
- A host's toolbar button may have a list: `ToolbarButton.menu` (`ToolbarMenuItem`), drawn as the toolbar's own lists; without `run` a click opens it, with `run` the button is split. `run` is now optional.

## 0.25.1 (2026-10-27)

- Fixed: below 600 px the toolbar's groups shrank and wrapped their buttons onto several lines, so the row measured less than it held and kept everything in it, without More (a few groups after `undo` and many host buttons). The row's groups now keep their width and their buttons on one line.

## 0.25.0 (2026-10-27)

Fixes and polish from the app's first look at 0.24.0. Nothing in the contract changes; a host's tests that read a toolbar button's name or a cell's note from `title` need another hook (below).

- Fixed: Trace Precedents in a sheet narrower than about 940 px redrew without end. The phone layout was decided by the grid's width, which the panel beside it narrows below 600 px, so the panel moved below the grid and back; it is now decided by the sheet's own width.
- Fixed: after a change the sheet reads again (a font size that fits rows, a number format, a structure operation), the grid showed default widths and heights and empty cells until the new windows arrived. The old windows and sizes now stay on screen until the first new window replaces them, and the scroll stays where it was.
- `SheetToolbar` below 600 px keeps in its row the items that fit, in order, and moves only the rest into More (there was room for more than Undo and the font style, and a host's few buttons went into More); with everything fitting there is no More button.
- `SheetToolbar` has Excel's ScreenTips: the name and the key below a button or box after 0.4 s of a resting mouse, the next at once, disabled buttons and the host's buttons included. The toolbar's controls no longer have `title`, so the browser's slow tooltip does not show on top; their text is in `data-tip` and `aria-label`. Bold, Italic and Underline name their keys (`Bold (Ctrl+B)`).
- A cell's note shows on hover as Excel shows it, in the note box, read-only, at once, and hides when the mouse leaves the cell; the cell's tooltip no longer carries the note. A host whose tests read the note from a cell's `title`, or a toolbar button's name from its `title`, reads `[data-xs-note-peek]` and `aria-label` or `data-tip` instead.

## 0.24.0 (2026-10-26)

AutoSum on the toolbar and the toolbar's items, as the app asked. Nothing breaks for a host; `./local` works with xlcalc 0.46.0 and later, its AutoSum with 0.59.0.

- AutoSum's list: the source's optional `autoSumFunctions` names the functions `autoSum` builds besides Sum (`AutoSumFunction`), and `autoSum` takes the function as an optional fourth argument; `SheetViewApi.autoSum(fn?)` enters Average, Count Numbers, Max or Min over the same guess, and `./local` builds all four in the workbook's locale. `SheetViewApi.insertFunction()` opens Insert Function, with or without the formula bar. `SheetViewState.canAutoSum` says AutoSum is available. A source with the earlier `autoSum` works as before.
- `SheetToolbar` has AutoSum with `sheet`: Σ enters Sum, its list Sum, the source's other functions and More Functions…. This changes the toolbar's default look: a host that passes `sheet` gets the new button.
- `SheetToolbar`'s `items` picks its groups and their order (`ToolbarGroup`, `TOOLBAR_GROUPS` the default) and adds the host's buttons (`ToolbarButton`), drawn as the toolbar's own and moved into its menu below 600 px. Without it the toolbar is drawn as before, AutoSum aside. In a wide toolbar the groups now wrap as one row.

## 0.23.0 (2026-10-26)

Over xlcalc 0.59.0, the parts of the app's list that waited for the library. Nothing breaks for a host; `./local` works with xlcalc 0.46.0 and later, its AutoSum and the workbook's font with 0.59.0.

- `SheetToolbar`: Accounting Number Format and Comma Style (Excel's built-in formats 44 and 43, in the workbook's locale), Increase Decimal and Decrease Decimal through xlcalc's `stepDecimals`, from the active cell's format, value and column width, over the selection. `SheetViewState` has `cellValue` and `columnWidth` for them.
- AutoSum: the source's optional `autoSum` (`SheetAutoSum`), Alt+= and `SheetViewApi.autoSum()` open the active cell's edit with `=SUM(range)` and the range selected; `./local` gives xlcalc's `getAutoSum`.
- `./local`: `SheetInfo.font` is the workbook's default font, Calibri 11 without one, the font in which xlcalc measures widths and `####`, so cells are drawn as measured rather than in the page's font (`docs/links-notes.md` says how a host serves Carlito under Calibri's name). The Name Manager's names are refused by xlcalc's own `setNames`. A font that fits rows to it (xlcalc's `fitted`) reads the sheets and windows again.

## 0.22.0 (2026-10-26)

Excel's commands from the Home, Insert, Formulas, Data, Review and View tabs that the sheet lacked. Nothing breaks for a host: every new source method, prop, event and method is optional. Built against xlcalc 0.58.0; `./local` works with xlcalc 0.46.0 and later.

- `SheetSource`: optional methods for what a workbook holds beside its cells' values, in Excel's terms: `clear` (Clear Formats, Clear All), `link` (Insert Link, Remove Hyperlinks), `note` (New Note, Edit Note, Delete Note), `names` and `setNames` (the Name Manager), `dependents` (Trace Dependents), `rules` and `setRules` (conditional formatting and data validation rules), `table` (Format as Table, Convert to Range) and `spelling` with `learnWord` (Spelling, Add to Dictionary). Their types are in `src/contract/workbook.ts`; formulas in them are in the source's locale. `SheetInfo` has `font`, the workbook's default font, and `zoom`, the sheet's saved zoom. `memorySource` has `clear`, `link`, `note`, `names` and `setNames`; both it and `./local` check spelling with the host's `SpellChecker` (the `spelling` option), and `./local` has every new method. `./local` checks the Name Manager's names with xlcalc's `validateNames`, does not list hidden names and keeps them.
- `SheetToolbar`: the Font and Font Size boxes (the `fonts` prop replaces the font list), Increase and Decrease Font Size, Top, Middle and Bottom Align, Increase and Decrease Indent, and Percent Style with Ctrl+Shift+%.
- Fill Down and Fill Right (Ctrl+D, Ctrl+R) through `structure`'s `copyRange`, as a single undo step; Show Formulas (Ctrl+`), a view setting of each sheet.
- Go To (F5, Ctrl+G) with the last four locations and the defined names; the Symbol dialog with Unicode subsets, the character code, recently used symbols and Special Characters.
- Zoom from 10% to 400%: Ctrl+wheel, a pinch, the status bar's slider and the Zoom dialog with Zoom to Selection; each sheet keeps its own, a sheet opens at the workbook's saved zoom unless the host's `zoom` prop names it, and the `zoom` event reports the user's changes. The Watch Window (the `watches` prop and event).
- Defined names: the Name Box goes to a name, shows the name of the selected range and lists the names; a new name typed in it defines one; Define Name, the Name Manager (Ctrl+F3) and names in Formula AutoComplete.
- Insert Hyperlink (Ctrl+K) and Edit Hyperlink, with Place in This Document and the defined names; Edit, Open and Remove Hyperlink in the cell menu; New, Edit and Delete Note in the cell menu and Shift+F2, with the `noteAuthor` prop; `clear` for Clear Formats and Clear All.
- The workbook's default font: a cell without a font of its own is drawn in `SheetInfo.font`, and the toolbar's Font and Font Size boxes show it (`state.font`).
- Trace Dependents and Remove Arrows; Create Table (Ctrl+T, Ctrl+L; Control+T on a Mac, where Control+L is Define Name) with Excel's names for the table and its columns, Convert to Range and Delete Table; Spelling (F7) over the host's dictionary.
- Conditional formatting: Highlight Cells Rules and Top/Bottom Rules with Excel's presets and Custom Format, Data Bars, Color Scales and Icon Sets, Clear Rules from the selection or the sheet, New Formatting Rule and the Rules Manager.
- Data Validation: Excel's dialog with Settings, Input Message and Error Alert, Clear All, and Excel's questions when the selection has several rules or cells without one.
- A paste that extends past the sheet's edge is refused with Excel's reason under xlcalc 0.56 and later too, whose rectangle helpers check their arguments strictly.

## 0.21.2 (2026-10-20)

The first public release. Earlier versions were internal builds and were not published to npm; their changes are folded into this entry, and the README and `docs/` describe the package as it is now. The internal builds were named `xlcalc-sheet`: a host built on them imports `@xlcalc/vue`, `@xlcalc/vue/local` and `@xlcalc/vue/style.css` instead (breaking for that host).

Contents:

- `SheetView`: a virtualized grid over a `SheetSource`, with the workbook's styles, number formats, conditional formats, merged ranges, tables, hyperlinks and notes. It has:
  - editing with Excel's keys;
  - the formula bar as in Excel: always an input (a click places the caret at the click, a drag selects; on read-only sheets the text can be copied), a single line expanded by its arrow or Ctrl+Shift+U, references colored like their range borders on the grid, and Ctrl+click (Cmd+click) on a reference going to its range (with the key held, the reference under the pointer is underlined and the pointer becomes a hand; on a touch screen a long press on it goes to it), with Excel's Cancel and Enter buttons next to it and Insert Function (fx: search, Excel's categories, the ten most recently used, each function's syntax and description);
  - Point mode with the mouse and the keys as measured in Excel (xlcalc's `refAt`: an arrow inserts a reference, enters the formula or does nothing; Cmd+arrow or Ctrl+arrow, Home and Page Down move the reference as on the sheet);
  - Formula AutoComplete and Excel's argument tooltip (the current argument in bold, from the source's `functionSignature`, for the call found by xlcalc's `callAt`);
  - fill and paste, Ctrl+Enter, undo and redo, and Excel's "We found a typo in your formula" dialog offering the source's correction of an unclosed formula;
  - selection, Ctrl+arrow as Excel's Range.End, frozen panes, the fill handle continuing series as Excel's AutoFill does (`fillRange`: 1, 2 → 3, 4, dates, Item1 → Item2; Ctrl or Option held on release for a series from a single number), and cut, copy and paste with Excel's clipboard text;
  - Find and Replace, data validation with Excel's dropdown, input message and alerts, the outline, the display filter and sort, the Trace Precedents panel, and context menus for cells, rows, columns and sheet tabs;
  - column widths and row heights by mouse (cells whose display changes with a new width are redrawn from `layout`'s changes), with AutoFit on a double click.
- List mode: the host's list on the sheet, with column titles the host sorts by (`sort`), drill-down cells (`cellDrill`), rows opened from the first column (`openRows`, `rowOpen`) and a totals row pinned to the bottom.
- `SheetToolbar` and `FormatDialog`: Excel's formatting toolbar, with Merge & Center and its dropdown (Merge Across, Merge Cells, Unmerge Cells) and Excel's warning before values are lost, and Excel's Format Cells dialog.
- `SheetView`'s `hostLinks` and `SheetToolbar`'s `hostDialog`: a host whose listener the component cannot see, such as a custom element's, still opens links (`openLink`) and its own Format Cells dialog (`formatCells`).
- The dialogs share one modal container: Esc and a click outside close Format Cells, Insert Function and the context menu's dialogs, and Tab stays inside every dialog. The primary buttons (Apply, OK) use the accent color and darken on hover. Inputs on touch screens have 16 px text, so iOS does not zoom into them.
- CSS variables `--xs-on-accent`, `--xs-shadow-pop`, `--xs-shadow-menu`, `--xs-shadow-modal`, `--xs-backdrop`, `--xs-z-menu`, `--xs-z-modal` and `--xs-z-alert`; `--xs-outline-bg` and `--xs-outline-button-bg` default to `--xs-bar-bg` and `--xs-bg`, and `--xs-font-size` applies to the formula bar, the menus and the filter funnel's list. Breaking for a host built on the internal builds: `SheetMessages.validationRetry` is removed (the alert's Retry uses `retry`), `--xs-fl-on-fg` is removed (use `--xs-on-accent`), and the classes `.xs-fmt-main`, `.xs-va-main`, `.xs-tb-swatch`, `.xs-fmt-swatch`, `.xs-dialog-back`, `.xs-ifn-back` and `.xs-va-back` are replaced by `.xs-button-main`, `.xs-swatch` and `.xs-modal-back`.
- The `SheetSource` contract (`src/contract/source.ts`): every feature beyond reading sheets and windows is an optional method, so a server-side source and an in-page source each provide what they support. An `invalid` outcome may carry `correction`, the formula Excel suggests for a typo. `functionSignature(name)` returns a function's arguments for Excel's argument tooltip while a formula is typed, and its description for Insert Function (`SheetFunctionSignature`). Fields the sheet never read were removed before the release (breaking for a host built on the internal builds): `visibleRows` returns `{ rows }`, a rejection carries no `cells` and no `busy` reason, Find's hits are `{ row, col }`, and `SheetWindow.bars` and `MemoryCell.bar` are replaced by `appearance`.
- `memorySource`: a source over an in-memory workbook, for examples and tests. Its display filter follows `./local`'s rules, its Find and Replace match as xlcalc's do (`findMatcher`, `findReplacer`), its outline groups come from xlcalc's `outlineGroups` and its outline commands from xlcalc's `outlineEdit`, with the same commands and rejections as `./local`, and its widths, heights and outline levels also accept an entire column or row as `C:C` or `5:5`.
- `@xlcalc/vue/local`: `localSource`, a source over an xlcalc workbook calculated in a Web Worker or on the page's main thread, which performs the grid's operations as Excel does, plus `openXlsx` and `saveXlsx`. The display filter, its sort and the filter funnel read only the columns they need from the session, once; selection totals come from the session's `summarizeRanges` without transferring the cells, and under a display filter cover only its rows; a request's signal stops the session's reads; the sort and the funnel order mixed values as Excel's SORT does, with text in the workbook's locale (xlcalc's `valueComparator`; the ru-RU order is the en-US one, as Excel for Windows sorts); a style set by `format` repaints its ranges instead of reloading the sheet. It requires `xlcalc` 0.46.0 or later, an optional peer dependency.
- The public types in `src/contract/` by area (`source`, `view`, `memory`, `messages`), behind `src/contract.ts`; `MemoryOutline`, the type of `MemorySheet.outline`, is exported.
- `docs/`: a quick start with the common uses, each a component that works as written (`docs/quick-start.md`), the API reference (`docs/api.md`) and a page for each feature.
- Interface texts in English by default and in Russian (`ruMessages`). The English texts use Excel's command names (Font Color, All Borders, Wrap Text) and the term workbook. Breaking for a host built on the internal builds that overrides them: the `SheetMessages` keys `fontColour`, `fillColour`, `pickColour`, `colourAuto`, `fmtColour`, `fmtColourCode` and `fmtColourBad` are renamed to `fontColor`, `fillColor`, `pickColor`, `colorAuto`, `fmtColor`, `fmtColorCode` and `fmtColorBad`.
- `example/spa.html`: an Excel-like app over `./local` that runs entirely in the browser, without a server.
- Without the window's `editText`, a cell opens for editing with xlcalc's `entryText` (`xlcalc/entry`) in the workbook's locale according to its code in `numberFormats`, as Excel's formula bar displays it; the module is a separate chunk, loaded when the first cell needs it, and until then the editor opens with the displayed text. `MemoryCell.numberFormat` sets the format codes of `memorySource`'s cells. Breaking for a host without `editText`: a number opens as General without `numberFormats`, and the apostrophe depends only on the workbook's locale.
- The Column Width box counts characters as Excel for Mac's does (`columnWidthToChars`, `columnWidthFromChars` of `xlcalc/text`): the default column is 8, and entering 8 restores it. A row height goes up to 409.5 points, both by dragging the row's edge and in the Row Height box.
- Formula AutoComplete finds the typed name with `nameAt` of `xlcalc/text`, after the workbook's list separator only (`,` in en-US, `;` in ru-RU).
- The grid renders by rows: a typed character, a move of the active cell or of the selection, a scroll and a cell change redraw only the rows they affect (5 to 9 ms in Chromium on a desktop, down from 15 to 25); repaints from one task go to the source as a single request, and a burst of source events requests the active cell's validation once.
- Ctrl+A, Ctrl+F and Ctrl+H work by the key's position on any keyboard layout, as the other shortcuts do; AltGr (Ctrl+Alt) types its character instead, so Ctrl+Alt+A no longer selects all.
- A horizontal scroll creates and patches only the columns it brings into view, and a cell is clipped once, at its content box: scrolling by two columns takes 17 ms in Chromium (down from 22), by four rows 8 ms (down from 11), and a typed character 4.8 ms (down from 5.5).
- A format change that the source repaints is not read a second time; after a structure change, the requested sheet is found by name case-insensitively, as Excel compares sheet names; a double click on the fill handle stops when another sheet is displayed instead of filling it.
- Built against xlcalc 0.54.2, whose English messages are rewritten in plain English (xlcalc 0.53.0). The outline commands of `memorySource` reject with the new texts, since the main entry point bundles `xlcalc/a1`: an Ungroup of rows in no group says `the rows 3:6 are not in a group`, a group's button says `the detail of row 8 is already shown`. `@xlcalc/vue/local` returns the texts of the `xlcalc` the host installs, the new ones from 0.53.0. A host that matches these texts rather than their meaning needs the new ones.
- The context menu's dialogs are labeled by their titles for screen readers. Nothing of a sheet outlives it: a search waiting for its pause, the filter's row list, an explain response, a long press, and the reads of the fill handle and the merge warning stop when the sheet is unmounted.
- The type declarations name each imported file with `.js` and import no stylesheet, so both entry points' types resolve with `moduleResolution` `bundler`, `node16` and `nodenext`, with `skipLibCheck` off.
- A new `source`, or the sheet's unmount, ends the previous workbook: its sheets, edit outcomes, corrections and rejections answered later are ignored, its alert closes, and its pending cells are no longer marked, so nothing of it is written or displayed in the new workbook. Of two reloads of one workbook answered in reverse order, the newer list of sheets stays.
- A refusal may carry `code`, the source's code for its reason: `memorySource` and `./local` give xlcalc's codes of the outline commands (`outline_deep`, `not_grouped`, `detail`), and `./local` those of the structure operations, so a host can react to a refusal without matching its text.
- At runtime the main entry point depends only on `vue` and does not include the engine. Its test keeps it under 400,000 characters (389,000 now); the engine would add hundreds of thousands.
