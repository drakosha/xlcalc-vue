# Changelog

## Unreleased

The first public release. The versions before it were internal builds and were not published to npm; their changes are folded into this entry, and the README with `docs/` describes the package as it is. The internal builds were named `xlcalc-sheet`: a host built on them imports `@xlcalc/vue`, `@xlcalc/vue/local` and `@xlcalc/vue/style.css` instead (breaking for that host).

What is in it:

- `SheetView`: a windowed grid over a `SheetSource`, with the book's styles, number formats, conditional formats, merged areas, tables, hyperlinks and notes; editing with Excel's keys, the formula bar as Excel's: always a field (a click puts the caret where it was pressed, a drag selects; read-only sheets let the text be copied), one line, expanded by its arrow or Control+Shift+U, with references coloured as the grid's frames and Ctrl+click (Cmd+click) on one going to its range (with the key held the reference under the mouse is underlined and the cursor a hand; on a touch screen a long press on one goes to it), Excel's Cancel and Enter beside it, and Insert Function (fx: search, Excel's categories, the ten used last, each function's syntax and description), Point mode by the mouse and the keys as Excel measured it (xlcalc's `refAt`: an arrow points, enters the formula or does nothing; Command or Control and an arrow, Home and Page Down move the reference as on the sheet), Formula AutoComplete and Excel's tip with a function's arguments (the current one in bold, from the source's `functionSignature`, at the call xlcalc's `callAt` finds), fill and paste, Control+Return, undo and redo, Excel's "We found a typo in your formula" window offering the source's correction of a formula left open; selection, Control and an arrow as Excel's Range.End, frozen panes, the fill handle going on with series as Excel's AutoFill (`fillRange`: 1, 2 → 3, 4, dates, Item1 → Item2; Ctrl or Option at the release for a series of one number), cut, copy and paste as Excel copies text, Find and Replace, data validation with Excel's dropdown, input message and alerts, the outline, the display filter and sort, the precedents' panel, menus of cells, lines and tabs, column widths and row heights by the mouse (the cells a new width shows otherwise drawn from `layout`'s changes) with AutoFit on a double click.
- List mode: a host's list on the sheet, its titles sorted by the host (`sort`), drill-down cells (`cellDrill`), rows opened from the first column (`openRows`, `rowOpen`) and a totals row held at the bottom.
- `SheetToolbar` and `FormatDialog`: Excel's formatting row, with Merge & Center and its list (Merge Across, Merge Cells, Unmerge Cells) and Excel's warning before values are lost, and its Format Cells dialog.
- The dialogs share one modal box: Esc and a press outside close Format Cells, Insert Function and the menu's dialogs, and Tab stays inside every dialog. The main buttons (Apply, OK) show the accent and darken on hover. Fields under a finger have 16 px text, so iOS does not zoom into them.
- CSS variables `--xs-on-accent`, `--xs-shadow-pop`, `--xs-shadow-menu`, `--xs-shadow-modal`, `--xs-backdrop`, `--xs-z-menu`, `--xs-z-modal` and `--xs-z-alert`; `--xs-outline-bg` and `--xs-outline-button-bg` follow `--xs-bar-bg` and `--xs-bg`, and `--xs-font-size` reaches the formula bar, the menus and the funnel's list. Breaking for a host built on the internal builds: `SheetMessages.validationRetry` is gone (the alert's Retry takes `retry`), `--xs-fl-on-fg` is gone (`--xs-on-accent`), and the classes `.xs-fmt-main`, `.xs-va-main`, `.xs-tb-swatch`, `.xs-fmt-swatch`, `.xs-dialog-back`, `.xs-ifn-back` and `.xs-va-back` gave way to `.xs-button-main`, `.xs-swatch` and `.xs-modal-back`.
- The `SheetSource` contract (`src/source.ts`): every feature beyond reading windows is an optional method, so a source on a server and one in the page give what they have. An `invalid` outcome may carry `correction`, the formula Excel offers for a typo. `functionSignature(name)` gives a function's arguments for Excel's tip while a formula is typed, and its description for Insert Function (`SheetFunctionSignature`). Fields the sheet never read were dropped before the release (breaking for a host built on the internal builds): `visibleRows` answers `{ rows }`, a refusal carries no `cells` and no `busy` reason, Find's hits are `{ row, col }`, and `SheetWindow.bars` with `MemoryCell.bar` gave way to `appearance`.
- `memorySource`: a source over a book held in memory, for an example or a test; its display filter shares `./local`'s rules, its Find and Replace match as xlcalc's (`findMatcher`, `findReplacer`), its outline groups are xlcalc's `outlineGroups` and its outline commands xlcalc's `outlineEdit`, the same commands and refusals as `./local`'s, and its widths, heights and outline levels take a line as `C:C` or `5:5` too.
- `@xlcalc/vue/local`: `localSource`, a source over an xlcalc book that computes in a Web Worker or in the page's thread and does what the grid asks as Excel does it, with `openXlsx` and `saveXlsx`. The display filter, its sort and funnel read only the columns they need from the session, once; selection totals come from the session's `summarizeRanges` without the cells, under a display filter over its rows; a request's signal stops the session's reads; the sort and the funnel order mixed values as Excel's SORT, text in the book's locale (xlcalc's `valueComparator`; ru-RU's order is en-US's, as Excel for Windows sorts); a look set by `format` repaints its ranges instead of reloading the sheet; it needs `xlcalc` 0.46.0 or later, an optional peer.
- `docs/`: the API reference (`docs/api.md`) and a page for each feature.
- The interface's texts in English by default and in Russian (`ruMessages`).
- `example/spa.html`: an app like Excel over `./local`, all in the browser, without a server.
- Without the window's `editText` a cell opens with xlcalc's `entryText` (`xlcalc/entry`) in the book's locale by its code in `numberFormats`, as Excel's formula bar shows it; the module is a chunk of its own, loaded when the first cell needs it, and the field opens with the shown text until then. `MemoryCell.numberFormat` gives `memorySource`'s cells their codes. Breaking for a host without `editText`: a number opens as General without `numberFormats`, and the apostrophe follows the book's locale alone.
- The Column Width box counts characters as Excel for Mac's does (`columnWidthToChars`, `columnWidthFromChars` of `xlcalc/text`): the default column is 8, and 8 gives it back. A row goes up to 409.5 points, by its edge and in the Row Height box.
- Formula AutoComplete finds the typed name with `nameAt` of `xlcalc/text`, after the book's list separator alone (`,` in en-US, `;` in ru-RU).
- The grid draws by rows: a typed character, a move of the active cell or of the selection, a scroll and a cell's change redraw only the rows they touch (5 to 9 ms in Chromium on a desktop, from 15 to 25); repaints of one task go to the source as one request, and a stream of the source's events asks the active cell's validation once.
- Control+A, Control+F and Control+H take the key's place on any layout, as the other shortcuts do; AltGr (Control+Alt) types its character there, so Control+Alt+A no longer selects all.
- A sideways scroll makes and patches only the columns it brings in, and a cell clips once, at its content box: a scroll by two columns 17 ms in Chromium (from 22), by four rows 8 ms (from 11), a typed character 4.8 ms (from 5.5).
- A format the source repaints is not read again besides; after a structure change the sheet asked for is found by its name in any case, as Excel names sheets; a double click on the fill handle stops when another sheet is shown instead of filling it.
- The menu's dialogs are named by their titles for a screen reader. Nothing of a sheet outlives it: a search waiting for its pause, the filter's list of rows, an explain answer, a long press and the reads of the fill handle and of the merge warning stop when it goes.
- The main entry depends at runtime on `vue` alone and carries no engine. Its test holds it under 400,000 characters (388,000 now): the engine would add hundreds of thousands.
